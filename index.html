ABOUT BLAQNEM

Nigerian rapper. Gwagwalada, Abuja. Music since 2020.

Blaqnem is a Nigerian rapper from Gwagwalada, Abuja, who began making music in 2020. His sound draws from hip-hop, rap, Afrodrill and Afrobeats, shaped by influences including Stormzy, Dave, Burna Boy, Damian Marley and Eminem.

His music is rooted in personal experience, storytelling, ambition and self-expression. Right now, Blaqnem is focused on putting more music into the world, building his catalogue and giving more people the chance to understand who he is and what he stands for.
